# Learvix demo run sheet

1. Sign in with the demo account.
2. Show the home dashboard and explain the peer-learning idea.
3. Open Matching and show compatible partners and skill overlap.
4. Open a partner profile and create an exchange.
5. Open Progress and show the exchange/session progress.
6. Open Notifications and show the exchange update.
7. Open Profile and demonstrate editing skills offered/wanted.
8. Briefly show SkillProof as the practical challenge concept.

Avoid claiming live video calling or automated AI scoring: those are not implemented as verified services in this build.
